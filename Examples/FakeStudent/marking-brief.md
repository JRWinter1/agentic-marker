# Module Marking Brief

## Module Details
- **Module Code**: BM5727
- **Assessment**: 001 (worth 100% of the overall module mark)
- **Level**: Level 5

## Learning Outcomes

- **LO1**: Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the essential components and theories associated with project management.
- **LO2**: Apply the principles of research and planning that provide the framework for project management as a methodology to deliver stakeholder requirements in a real or simulated project.
- **LO3**: Apply knowledge and understanding of the control stage of a project including milestone, gates and incorporate how risk assessment modules are critical to project planning and control.
- **LO4**: Reflect critically on project management planning and focusing on the efficacy of the project planning and management to meet the stakeholder requirements, and on elements such as quality, cost, time, and risk.
